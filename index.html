🃏 User Guide

Shan Koe Mee · ရှမ်းကိုးမီး

Objective

Play against the Dealer (Banker). Get as close to 9 points as possible and beat the Dealer's hand.

Card Values

CardValue
Ace (A)1
2 – 9Face value (2=2, 9=9)
10, J, Q, K0

Add your card values and take only the last digit (mod 10).

Example: 7 + 8 = 15 → 5 points · K + 9 = 9 → 9 points

Multipliers (Payout Size)

Multipliers affect how much you win or lose — not who wins.

Winner Determination

Always follow this order:

  • Step 1: Higher POINT score wins (9 > 8 > 7 ...).
  • Step 2: If points tie, higher face value of highest card wins (K > Q > J > 10 > ... > A).
  • Step 3: If face value ties, higher suit of that card wins.
  • Tie: Bet is returned (push).

Suit Hierarchy

From greatest to least:

♠ Spade 1st
♥ Heart 2nd
♦ Diamond 3rd
♣ Club 4th

All In 🔥

Click ALL IN to bet every chip you have in one round. If you win, you win big. If you lose, you lose everything.

Game Flow

  • Bet: Enter your wager and click DEAL.
  • Deal: You and the Dealer each receive 2 cards. Dealer's second card is hidden.
  • See Your Points: Your current point total and hand type display above your cards.
  • Naturals: A natural 8 (Chit) or 9 (Koe) reveals instantly and skips to showdown.
  • Your Turn: Choose HIT (draw 1 more card, max 3 total) or STAND (keep your hand).
  • Dealer Turn: Dealer reveals their hidden card and hits if score is below 4.
  • Showdown: Hands compared using Points → Face Value → Suit.

Payouts

If you WIN: You receive your bet back + (Bet × Your Multiplier).

Example: Bet 10 with a 2× hand → get back 30 chips (10 stake + 20 winnings).

If you LOSE: You pay Bet × Dealer's Multiplier.

Example: Bet 10, Dealer has 3× hand → you lose 30 chips.

Push: Tie — your bet is returned.
